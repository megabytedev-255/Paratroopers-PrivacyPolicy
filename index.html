Privacy Policy

Paratroopers: Sky Defense, by Megabyte Digital (Jaskaran Singh)
Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Paratroopers: Sky Defense ("the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean Megabyte Digital. Questions? Email megabytedigital255@gmail.com.

In short
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no accounts of its own.
  • Your scores and settings are saved on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google Play Games, your best scores are sent to Google's leaderboards and shown publicly with your Play Games name and avatar.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not run our own servers.

1. Information the game uses

Stored only on your device

The game saves your best score and best wave for each difficulty, your chosen difficulty, your settings (sound, music, vibration, graphics, aiming) and whether you have completed the tutorial. This file stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Google Play Games Services (leaderboards)

The game uses Google Play Games Services to provide leaderboards. Signing in is optional. When you are signed in:

  • Your best score for each difficulty is sent to Google and shown on public leaderboards.
  • Your Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar are used by Google to show who holds each score.
  • Google's software also collects diagnostic and usage data to keep its service working and improve it.

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We can only see what Play Games shows on the leaderboards. Who can see your Play Games profile and scores is controlled by your Play Games profile settings.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you.

Device permissions

The game uses the vibration permission for haptic feedback during play. You can turn vibration off in the game's Settings. The game uses the internet only for Google Play Games.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. The only party involved is Google, which runs Google Play Games Services and stores leaderboard data. Leaderboard scores, with your Play Games name and avatar, are visible to other players according to your Play Games settings. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. How long it is kept

  • On-device data is kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Leaderboard data is kept by Google until you delete it or your Play Games account.
  • Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

5. Your choices, rights and deleting your data

  • Don't sign in to Google Play Games, and no information leaves your device.
  • Delete on-device data: Android Settings → Apps → Paratroopers → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Delete or hide leaderboard data: use your Play Games profile (you can delete the data for this game or your whole profile) or Google My Account.
  • Contact us at megabytedigital255@gmail.com for any other request, such as access to or deletion of emails you sent us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada, or certain US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict its use. Because we do not hold your game data ourselves, most requests are handled through the Google tools above; we will help with anything else. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or share personal information for targeted advertising.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Use of Google Play Games is subject to Google's own age requirements. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Data sent to Google Play Games is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we keep the information involved to a minimum.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it uses information (for example, by adding ads), we will update this policy and its date before that update is released.
